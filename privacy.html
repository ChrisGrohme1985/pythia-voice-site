The Pythia Voice app

What the app processes. When you dictate, your voice is recorded briefly on your device and sent to the Groq service (Groq, Inc., USA) with your personal API key. There it is converted to text and cleaned up. The recognized text is then inserted into the text field you are writing in. Pythia Voice does not store the recording. The history text and your dictionary stay on your device only; you can delete them in the settings.

Accessibility service. The service is used only to show the dictation button above the keyboard and to insert the dictated text into the active text field. Only the text field you are writing in is read. Other screen content is neither read nor stored. In password fields the button is hidden.

Your API key stays on your device only. We do not receive it.
