The Pythia Voice app

What the app processes. When you dictate, your voice is recorded briefly on your device and sent to the Groq service (Groq, Inc., USA) with your personal API key. There it is converted to text and cleaned up. The recognized text is then inserted into the text field you are writing in. Pythia Voice does not store the recording. The history text and your dictionary stay on your device only; you can delete them in the settings.

Accessibility service. The service is used only to show the dictation button above the keyboard and to insert the dictated text into the active text field. Only the text field you are writing in is read. Other screen content is neither read nor stored. In password fields the button is hidden.

Your API key stays on your device only. We do not receive it.

License and trial. The trial status (start time, number of dictated words) and an activated license key are stored locally and, to restore them after a reinstall, backed up through Google Block Store with your Google account (if device backup is on). When buying through Google Play, Google processes the payment.

Disclosure. Audio and text go only to Groq, and only when you dictate. Groq’s privacy terms (groq.com) apply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you trigger dictation yourself).

Voice messages. With the “voice messages” module you pick an audio file or share it from another app. The file is stored temporarily in the app’s cache, sent to Groq with your Groq key to be converted to text, and then deleted immediately. The text is only displayed and not stored unless you copy or share it yourself. A message may contain voices and content of third parties; you are responsible for being allowed to process it.

Storage of purchase data (Firebase)

For order handling and bookkeeping we store for each purchase: date, amount, e-mail address, invoice number, the Stripe reference of the payment, and fee and refund. This data is kept in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Google Cloud EMEA Ltd., Ireland, and Google LLC, USA) and is accessible only to us. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR (performance of the contract and statutory retention duties). Retention follows the statutory periods, after which we delete the data. Transfers to the USA rely on standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
