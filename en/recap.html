Find the right spot with your thumb

A long meeting is hard to search. Pythia Recap shows the whole meeting and a close-up of two minutes, with large markers you can actually hit.

  • Markers are 48 dp touch targets: tap to jump to five seconds before
  • Drag the close-up to scrub precisely, with a magnifier that shows time and text
  • Snaps to markers, vibrates when you pass one, speed from 1× to 2×

Online meetings: how to get the sound

Android does not let apps record Zoom, Teams or Meet directly. These are the ways to get an audio track:

  • Zoom: record locally and share the audio-only file (m4a).
  • Microsoft Teams: share the recording from OneDrive or SharePoint.
  • Google Meet: share the recording from Google Drive.
  • Windows or Mac: record the system sound with OBS, Audacity or BlackHole.
  • Nothing else works: put the phone next to the laptop (laptop mode).

Always tell everyone that you record and get their consent first. Recording without consent can be a criminal offence.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. You need the app and your own free Groq key. We run no accounts and no servers for your meetings.

What does Groq cost?

Groq offers a free tier that is enough for many meetings. Its limits can pause very long jobs for a while. Beyond that, usage costs a few cents per hour of audio.

Can it record Zoom or Teams directly?

Android does not allow that for apps. Use the recording of the meeting software, the system sound on your computer, or laptop mode. The guides in the app explain each way.

Does it detect who is speaking?

Not automatically. You assign speakers in a few taps, and one choice covers everything until the next speaker change. Markers set while recording make it faster.

What happens to my data?

Meetings are stored on your phone. For transcription and summary, audio and text are sent to Groq in the USA with your key. Groq does not keep them by default and may log requests for up to 30 days.

Which languages work?

Speech recognition works in more than 90 languages and detects the language automatically. The summary can be written in the language you choose.
