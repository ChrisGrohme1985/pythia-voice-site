Frequently asked questions

What does Pythia Voice cost?

After 7 free days (or 3,000 words), €4.99 a month or €39.99 a year through Google Play. That covers dictation, voice messages and speech recognition. You can cancel any time in Google Play; the subscription then runs until the end of the paid period.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. Recognition runs on our server. If you already have your own Groq key, you can enter it in the settings.

How do voice messages work?

Open the menu of a voice message in WhatsApp or another messenger, choose Share and pick Pythia Voice. The app transcribes it and shows the text split by minute, ready to copy or share.

Where do my recordings go?

Audio and text go through our server to our AI provider to be transcribed and cleaned up. Neither our server nor the app stores them, and we do not pass them on to anyone else.

Why does the app need Accessibility?

Only then can the button appear above the keyboard and the text land in the field you are typing in. Only that text field is read.
Video: the Accessibility service – why and how to turn it on (1 min)

I already bought Pythia Voice once. What changes?

Nothing. Your license stays valid and keeps working with your own Groq key as before. You do not need a subscription.

Does it work offline?

No. Recognition needs an internet connection.

Is there an iPhone or Windows version?

Not yet. Pythia Voice is Android only for now.

How long can a voice message be?

Files up to 4 MB work, which is plenty for long voice messages (WhatsApp messages are very small). A three-minute message takes only seconds.
